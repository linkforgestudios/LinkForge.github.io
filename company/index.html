About Us

LinkForge Studios began in 2023 out of a passion for pixel art gaming and nostalgia for interactive storytelling. What began with doodles and scribbles in the middle of the night turned into an extensive conception of what it means to make games that are as personal as they are fun to play. From the get-go, the intention is to fuse artistry with craft to make something so memorable it can't help but be unforgettable.

We understand that games are so much more than game mechanics, visuals, sound and so on. They are journeys, they are stories of emotions and feelings constructed by the framework of interface. That's why we honor every opportunity presented to us for the artistry within. From a strange concept to the smallest detail refinement and pitching whether players will embrace the experience, we view development as both a craft and a discussion.

On a personal level, LinkForge Studios is about community. We rely on feedback, collaboration, transparency, and player love for our final products. The more we share our process and welcome community input throughout development stages, the more cohesive and natural the product becomes for those whom it's intended.

That's why we get to know our projects inside and out - speed is not of the essence. Passion is. The faster we try to create something with hopes of completing a project or cashing in on trends, the more forced a product will become. We don't shy away from complicated concepts. We lean into them because every step we take is a lesson learned that will get us ever closer to making better games, but never better than the previous.

This is only the beginning of LinkForge Studios. As we proceed and expand, we will love to give life to new concepts - games that inspire interest, engagement, inquiry and exploration even after the game is over. The forge is heated and we have so much more to go.
